Billing preferences & numbering

How bill prefix and zero-padding digits work

Prefix INV- + zero-padding 4 renders bill numbers as INV-0031. The next number always continues from the highest used number, so deleting bills never re-issues numbers. The same scheme drives duplicate, convert and repeat-bill cloning via BillingService.nextBillNo(settings).

How to add & use custom fonts

Pick a Google Font by name (or paste its URL) — it downloads and registers for the designer, previews and PDF export. Local files (.ttf/.otf) load from disk and are embedded into PDFs when used, so documents print identically on any machine.
